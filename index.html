Services

The difference between conventional research and EmotionMetrics research is not in the type of questions we ask: it is in the layer of reality we can access. While traditional methods capture what people say, our AI agent swarm reads what they feel.

Emotional Listening with AI

Detects in real time the emotion behind every response: confidence, doubt, genuine enthusiasm, unexpressed resistance. We reach where others cannot because we listen in a way others cannot.

Multimodal Emotional Analysis

Analyzes facial expressions, vocal tone, body language and verbal content in a coordinated way, crossing signals to detect inconsistencies between what is said and what is felt.

MVP Testing & Agile Cycles

Emotional feedback in hours, not weeks. Integrate emotional evidence in every agile development sprint: what generates attraction, doubt, abandonment and why.

AI for Agile Methodologies

Learn faster. Decide with more precision. Scale further. Our AI-first approach converts your users' emotions into precise data in the time of a sprint, accelerating the organizational learning cycle.

Continuous Intelligence

From isolated studies to permanent listening. A continuous intelligence system that monitors perceptions, emotions and expectations of your stakeholders in real time, anticipating changes before they become crises.

Educational Research

What students feel about their institution, their teacher and their learning experience. Emotional intelligence for institutional and classroom management based on real evidence.

Opinion Polling & Positioning

Methodology

At EmotionMetrics we operate under an AI-first methodology where artificial intelligence is part of the core of our processes, not an auxiliary tool.

AI-First Approach

AI is the infrastructure on which all work is built. Real-time analysis with a depth that surpasses any manual methodology.

AI Agent Swarm

Multiple specialized agents work in parallel: one conducts the interview, others simultaneously analyze expressions, voice and language. Multidimensional understanding in real time.

Multimodal Emotional Analysis

We integrate facial expressions, vocal tone, body language and verbal content simultaneously to build a high-fidelity emotional reading.

Project Flow

Co-design → parallel execution → real-time monitoring → decision-oriented executive presentation. Results in hours, not weeks.
